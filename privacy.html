Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Rumah Rapi Studio ("the App") is a personal tool operated by Muhammad Ridwan Ali (Indonesia) for his own TikTok account @tipsrapi. It is not offered as a public service.

TikTok integrations

The App uses TikTok Login Kit to authorize the creator's own account and the TikTok Content Posting API to upload videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts. Requested scopes: user.info.basic and video.upload. The App does not publish posts directly; the creator reviews and posts each draft manually in TikTok.

Data accessed

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored locally on the creator's own computer only.
  • Video files created by the creator, which are sent to TikTok for upload.

The App does not run a public server, does not collect data from other TikTok users, and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. It is sent only to TikTok as required to perform uploads.

Retention

Tokens are kept locally only while the integration is in use and are deleted when no longer needed or when access is revoked. Uploaded content is governed by TikTok's own policies.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (Manage app permissions).
